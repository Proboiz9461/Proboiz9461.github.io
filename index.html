About

Scholar. Mentor.
Innovator.

Dr. Rajeev Kumar Chauhan is a distinguished academic with a passion for bridging the gap between theoretical computer science and real-world applications. His work spans across Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Natural Language Processing, and emerging technologies.

With over two decades of teaching and research experience, Dr. Chauhan has mentored hundreds of students and guided numerous research scholars to doctoral completion. His interdisciplinary approach brings together computer science, mathematics, and domain expertise to tackle complex problems.

His contributions to academia include peer-reviewed publications in international journals, conference presentations, and collaborative research projects that have made a tangible impact in the field.
